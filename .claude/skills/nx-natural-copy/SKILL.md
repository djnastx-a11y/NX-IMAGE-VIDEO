# NX Natural Copy

## Quality rules
- Preserve the user's voice, facts, approved offers, dates, venue and contact details. Do not invent claims, testimonials, revenue, tickets or promotions.
- Prefer clear French, active verbs, concise informative sentences and natural variations in rhythm.
- Remove formulaic introductions, fake enthusiasm, repeated superlatives, vague promises and lists of benefits with no evidence.
- Avoid long dash punctuation, meaningless metaphors and stock AI phrasing.
- Use concrete action and detail when justified by source material. Keep promotional text grounded.
- Never add hashtags, emojis or marketing urgency unless specifically wanted.
- Treat legal, price or availability claims as needing verification. Keep draft vs published status explicit.
- For email outreach, use a validated recipient, an actual observation, a relevant proposal and an appropriate opt-out. Do not send.
- Proofread names, spelling, dates, accents and French typography before delivery.

## Two passes
1. Editorial pass: remove filler and awkward patterns without changing meaning.
2. Evidence pass: compare each factual sentence with the brief and supplied sources; flag unsupported text.

## Result
Produce the revised copy first. Briefly explain material factual changes if any. Do not claim publication, sending or approval.

## Quality examples
- A short DJ booking email that states availability and experience already verified.
- A natural Instagram caption for an Australia Street event using the correct date and offers.
- A clear sales page for NX Studio without claiming features that still exist only in code.
